Greenpower Receiver Agent — Setup

This installs a small background app that watches for the Greenpower LoRa
receiver over USB and forwards telemetry to the live dashboard. It asks
for your permission (a Windows notification) every time before forwarding
anything — nothing goes live silently.

Before you continue:

  - Requires Node.js (LTS) already installed — https://nodejs.org
    Setup will check for this and let you know if it's missing.
  - Requires an internet connection during setup (installs a few small
    dependencies).
  - No admin rights needed — this installs to your own user folder and
    registers itself to start automatically only for your account.

After setup finishes, day-to-day use is just: plug in the receiver, click
Yes on the notification. Nothing else to run.
